# Day 08 - Scrum

## What is Scrum?

Scrum is the most widely used Agile framework. It helps teams deliver software in short development cycles called Sprints.

A Sprint typically lasts between 1 and 4 weeks.

---

## Scrum Roles

### Product Owner
- Defines product requirements.
- Prioritizes the Product Backlog.
- Represents customer needs.

### Scrum Master
- Facilitates Scrum ceremonies.
- Removes blockers.
- Ensures Scrum practices are followed.

### Development Team
Includes:
- Developers
- QA Engineers
- Designers
- DevOps Engineers

---

## Scrum Ceremonies

### Sprint Planning
The team selects work for the upcoming Sprint.

### Daily Stand-up
A 15-minute meeting where team members discuss:
- What they completed yesterday
- What they will work on today
- Any blockers

### Sprint Review
Completed work is demonstrated to stakeholders for feedback.

### Sprint Retrospective
The team discusses improvements for future Sprints.

---

## Scrum Artifacts

- Product Backlog
- Sprint Backlog
- Increment

---

## QA Responsibilities in Scrum

- Review user stories.
- Write test cases.
- Perform testing during the Sprint.
- Report defects.
- Verify bug fixes.
- Collaborate with developers and Product Owner.

---

## Key Takeaway

Scrum promotes teamwork, continuous delivery, and early defect detection through regular collaboration and short development cycles.
