## Description
The comments section does not open after clicking the comment icon on an article's detail page. As a result, users are unable to view or interact with the article's comments.
---
## Preconditions
- User has navigated to https://www.goal.com/en
- An article displaying the comment icon is visible on screen
---
## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open https://www.goal.com/en
2. Navigate to any article displaying the comment icon.
3. Click the comment icon.
4. Click on other articles.
---
## Expected Result
The comments section should open and display the article's comments.
---
## Actual Result
The comments section does not open after clicking the comment icon. Nothing happens, and the comments cannot be viewed.
---
## Reproducibility
Always (100%) - Every Time
