# Key Concepts Learned

## Defect Management

Defect Management is the process of identifying, documenting, tracking, fixing, retesting, and closing software defects. It ensures bugs are managed systematically until they are resolved.

## Bug Life Cycle

A software bug passes through different stages from discovery to closure.

Typical workflow:

New → Assigned → Open → Fixed → Retest → Closed

Other possible statuses include:

- Reopened
- Rejected
- Duplicate
- Deferred

## Severity vs Priority

I reinforced my understanding that:

- **Severity** describes how much a bug affects the application's functionality.
- **Priority** indicates how quickly the bug should be fixed based on business impact.

Although related, they are not always the same.

# Biggest Lesson

A bug report should provide enough detail that another tester or developer can reproduce the issue without needing additional clarification.

A clear title, accurate reproduction steps, expected result, actual result, and supporting evidence are essential parts of a high-quality bug report.
