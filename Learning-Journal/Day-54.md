## Challenges

I initially confused load testing with stress testing and misunderstood the purpose of a performance baseline.

After reviewing the concepts, I understood that load testing evaluates an expected workload, while stress testing investigates behavior beyond normal operating conditions.

I also learned that a satisfactory average response time does not automatically mean all performance criteria have passed.

## Key Takeaways

1. Performance testing requires measurable requirements.
2. Throughput is calculated as requests divided by elapsed time.
3. Average response time and p95 provide different information.
4. Failed-request rates must be compared with their acceptance criteria.
5. QA conclusions should reflect the available measurements and evidence.
