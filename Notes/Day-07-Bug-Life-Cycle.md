# What is the Bug Life Cycle?

The Bug Life Cycle describes the stages a defect passes through from the moment it is reported until it is closed.

---

# Bug Life Cycle

New

↓

Assigned

↓

Open

↓

Fixed

↓

Retest

↓

Closed

---

# Additional Bug Statuses

## Reopened

The bug still exists after being marked as fixed.

---

## Rejected

The reported issue is not considered a valid defect.

Possible reasons:

- Works as designed
- Cannot reproduce
- Invalid report

---

## Duplicate

The defect has already been reported by another tester.

---

## Deferred

The development team decides to postpone fixing the defect until a future release.

---

# Importance

Understanding the Bug Life Cycle helps QA engineers track defects efficiently and communicate clearly with developers.
