# What is Defect Management?

Defect Management is the process of identifying, documenting, tracking, prioritising, fixing, verifying, and closing software defects throughout the Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC).

The objective is to ensure defects are resolved efficiently before the software is released to end users.

---

# Defect Management Process

1. Defect Identification
2. Defect Reporting
3. Defect Review
4. Defect Assignment
5. Defect Fixing
6. Retesting
7. Defect Closure

---

# Importance of Defect Management

- Improves software quality.
- Helps developers resolve issues efficiently.
- Prevents duplicate bug reports.
- Tracks the progress of reported defects.
- Improves communication between QA engineers and developers.

---

# QA Engineer's Responsibilities

- Identify defects accurately.
- Write clear and reproducible bug reports.
- Assign the correct severity and priority.
- Retest fixed defects.
- Close or reopen defects when necessary.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Every defect should be properly documented.
- A clear defect management process improves team productivity.
- Effective communication between QA and developers leads to faster bug resolution.
