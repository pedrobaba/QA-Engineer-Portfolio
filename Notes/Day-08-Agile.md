# Day 08 - Agile

## What is Agile?

Agile is a software development methodology that delivers software in small, incremental releases called iterations or sprints. It focuses on collaboration, customer feedback, and adapting quickly to changing requirements.

---

## Agile Principles

- Deliver working software frequently.
- Welcome changing requirements.
- Encourage collaboration between business and technical teams.
- Focus on customer satisfaction.
- Continuously improve through feedback.

---

## Advantages of Agile

- Faster software delivery
- Better communication among team members
- Continuous customer feedback
- Higher product quality
- Easier adaptation to changes

---

## Agile vs Waterfall

| Agile | Waterfall |
|--------|-----------|
| Iterative development | Sequential development |
| Flexible | Difficult to change requirements |
| Continuous testing | Testing usually happens at the end |
| Frequent releases | One major release |

---

## QA Engineer's Role in Agile

- Participate in Sprint Planning.
- Review user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Design test cases.
- Execute manual and automated tests.
- Report and verify bugs.
- Attend Daily Stand-ups.
- Participate in Sprint Reviews and Retrospectives.

---

## Key Takeaway

Agile encourages continuous testing and collaboration, allowing QA Engineers to contribute throughout the software development lifecycle instead of only testing at the end.
