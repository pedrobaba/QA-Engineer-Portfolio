# Day 10 – Test Estimation

## What is Test Estimation?

Test Estimation is the process of predicting the time, effort, resources, and cost required to complete software testing activities.

It helps QA teams plan testing efficiently and deliver projects on time.

---

## Why Test Estimation is Important

- Improves project planning.
- Helps allocate resources.
- Sets realistic deadlines.
- Reduces project risks.
- Improves communication with stakeholders.

---

## Factors Affecting Test Estimation

- Project size
- Application complexity
- Number of test cases
- Team experience
- Available resources
- Testing environment
- Time constraints
- Project risks

---

## Common Estimation Techniques

### Expert Judgment

Estimations are based on the experience of senior QA Engineers.

---

### Analogous Estimation

Uses data from previous projects with similar requirements.

---

### Three-Point Estimation

Uses three values:

- Optimistic Estimate
- Most Likely Estimate
- Pessimistic Estimate

---

### Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

Breaks testing activities into smaller tasks before estimating effort.

---

## Example

Testing Activities:

- Requirement Analysis – 4 Hours
- Test Case Design – 8 Hours
- Test Execution – 12 Hours
- Bug Reporting – 5 Hours
- Regression Testing – 6 Hours

Total Estimated Time:
35 Hours

---

## Key Takeaway

Accurate Test Estimation helps QA teams deliver quality software within the expected time while making efficient use of available resources.
