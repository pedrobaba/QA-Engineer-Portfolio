# 🧠 What Are Test Metrics?

Test metrics are measurements used to evaluate testing activities, progress, quality, coverage, and results.

In simple terms:

> **Test metrics are data used to measure testing.**

Example:

```text
Total Test Cases: 100
Executed: 90
Passed: 75
Failed: 15
Blocked: 10
