### Practice Calculations

- Total estimated effort: 9 hours.
- Six hours of effort at two hours per day: 3 working days.
- Two testers available for three hours each per day: 6 hours of combined daily capacity.
- Twelve hours of effort at that combined capacity: 2 working days.

### Resource Note

The calculations and scenarios in this section are learning exercises. They are not measurements from a live project.
